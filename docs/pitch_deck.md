# Blizkperse Pitch - Monad Blitz Denver

## 1. The Hook
**"Distributing crypto payments should be painless. Today, it’s anything but."**

## 2. The Problem: "The Cold Start"
Event organizers and DAOs want to send prizes and grants, but they hit a wall:
*   **Onboarding Friction**: Recipients often don't have wallets or gas tokens.
*   **The Waiting Game**: Organizers chase users for addresses. Funds sit stuck in multisigs.
*   **Privacy Leaks**: Everyone sees exactly how much each person got.

## 3. The Solution: Blizkperse
**We solve the cold start problem by reversing the flow.**
Instead of organizers pushing funds to an address, users **subscribe** to receive them. This works for **any token** (USDC, MON, Memes).

*   **Social Login**: Login with Google/Twitter. Wallets are generated instantly (Embedded).
*   **Subscription Model**: Recipients "Join" an Organizer's list with one click.
*   **Bulk Payouts**: Organizers select 1 or 100 subscribers and pay them all in **one transaction**.

## 4. Go-To-Market (GTM)
**Hackathons are just the start.**
We are launching with **Hackathons & Grants** as our "Beachhead Market" because the pain is acute.
*   **Phase 1**: Hackathons, Bounties, DAO Grants.
*   **Phase 2**: Payroll, Social Dividends, Creator Payouts.
*   **Phase 3**: General Peer-to-Peer Payments.

## 4. The Secret Sauce (Tech)
*   **Privacy First (ZK)**: Powered by **Zero-Knowledge proofs**. The network sees a bulk deposit, but the individual recipient's claim amount is private.
*   **Monad Speed**: "Venmo" style payments need sub-second finality. We use Monad’s high throughput to make mass-payouts feel instant.
*   **Non-Custodial Escrow**: Funds are safe in the contract until the rightful owner claims them.

## 5. The Bigger Picture (Agentic Future)
**We aren't just building for humans. We're building for Agents.**
The Blizkperse architecture is designed to be the payout layer for AI Agents (like **OpenClaw**).
*   **Today**: A DAO manager clicks "Pay".
*   **Tomorrow**: An AI Agent verifies a pull request and triggers the payout autonomously.

## 6. Closing
"Blizkperse makes crypto payments feel like a consumer app. Fast. Private. Painless.
**Built on Monad.**"

# Blizkperse Pitch - Monad Blitz Denver

## 1. The Hook
**"On-chain payments have a fatal flaw: They leak your entire business strategy."**

## 2. The Problem: "Reverse Engineering"
When a DAO or Company pays contributors on-chain, they dox themselves:
*   **Competitor Intel**: Rivals can reverse-engineer your payroll, see who your top talent is, and poach them.
*   **Salary Transparency**: Employees can calculate exactly what their peers make.
*   **Vendor Leaks**: The world knows exactly how much you pay for services.
*   **The Result**: Organizations are forced to use CEXs or mixed off-chain methods to hide their flows.

## 3. The Solution: Blizkperse
**Confidential Payouts for the Consumer Era.**
We block reverse-engineering by making the payment amount and recipient private.

*   **Public Deposit**: The network sees a bulk deposit from the Payer.
*   **Private Claim**: The recipient withdraws funds using a **Zero-Knowledge Proof**. No link between the sender and the receiver's new wallet.
*   **Subscription Model**: Users sub to an Organizer, enabling the payout flow without doxing their main address.

## 4. Go-To-Market (GTM)
**Hackathons are just the start.**
We are launching with **Hackathons & Grants** as our "Beachhead Market" because the pain is acute (Grants are often public but recipients want privacy).
*   **Phase 1**: Hackathons, Bounties, DAO Grants.
*   **Phase 2**: Payroll, Social Dividends, Creator Payouts.
*   **Phase 3**: General Peer-to-Peer Payments.

## 5. The Bigger Picture (Agentic Future)
**We aren't just building for humans. We're building for Agents.**
The Blizkperse architecture is designed to be the payout layer for AI Agents (like **OpenClaw**).
*   **Today**: A DAO manager clicks "Pay".
*   **Tomorrow**: An AI Agent verifies a pull request and triggers the confidential payout autonomously.

## 6. Closing
"Blizkperse makes crypto payments feel like a consumer app. Fast. Private. Painless.
**Built on Monad.**"
